## 1. What the UAE AI stack actually is

Not a ministry plus a model. A vertically integrated sovereign machine, almost all under Abu Dhabi, much of it chaired by or reporting through **Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed** (national security adviser, G42 chair).

### Policy and capital

| Layer | Vehicle | What it is (as of Aug 2026) |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy | [UAE National AI Strategy 2031](https://u.ae) (2017; first AI minister Omar Al Olama) | Eight objectives; claimed AI contribution AED 335bn / ~20% of *non-oil* GDP by 2031. Do **not** confuse with oil *fiscal* share (A7). |
| Apex (Abu Dhabi) | Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Technology Council (AIATC), Jan 2024 | Tahnoon-chaired coordinating body |
| Capital | **MGX** (Mar 2024, Mubadala + G42, AIATC) | ~$100bn target AUM. Equity in OpenAI, xAI, Databricks; co-funder of US Stargate ($500bn vehicle, Jan 2025); consortium with BlackRock GIP on Aligned Data Centers (~$40bn, Oct 2025); co-investor with Microsoft/BlackRock in a ~$30bn AI-infra fund. France hedge: UAE–France AI framework, tens of $bn into French DCs + Mistral |
| US hinge | Microsoft $1.5bn in G42 (Apr 2024) + $15.2bn UAE spend 2023–29 | Condition: G42 ripped Chinese kit (Huawei etc.). BIS later moved UAE toward Country Group A; G42/Core42 got time-limited approved-entity status. Chip deal (May 2025 Trump visit): up to **500k advanced Nvidia chips/year**; ~100k for G42, rest for US hyperscalers onshore |
| US campus | **UAE–US AI Acceleration Partnership** (May 2025) | 5 GW campus, ~19.2 km² (~10 sq mi), Abu Dhabi |

### Models and talent

- **TII / ATRC** — Falcon family (Falcon-180B 2023; Falcon Arabic / Falcon-H1 2025–26). $300m Falcon Foundation (2024)
- **MBZUAI** — first graduate AI university (Masdar City, 2020); undergrad 2025; Institute of Foundation Models. **K2 Think** (32B, Sep 2025) then **K2 Think V2** (70B, Jan 2026) with G42 + Cerebras. Open-weight reasoning play
- **Jais** — Inception Arabic LLM (2023), Jais 2 trained on ~600bn Arabic tokens
- Talent claims: 450k “coders” vs 300k target; treat as a marketing KPI

### The physical bet: Stargate UAE

- Announced 22 May 2025 ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-stargate-uae/)): first international Stargate; partners G42, Oracle, Nvidia, Cisco, SoftBank
- **1 GW cluster** inside a **5 GW UAE–US campus**; first **200 MW** targeted Q3 2026
- Khazna CCO (Aug 2026): two 100 MW buildings enclosed, kit on roofs, work continued through the Iran war; existing Khazna sites stayed up
- **Epoch AI satellite timeline** (updated 21 Aug 2026) — this is already the OSINT prototype:
  - 9 Jun 2025: land clearing, buildings 1–2
  - 16 Nov 2025: 1–2 partly up; no evidence of more buildings
  - Median: 200 MW by Dec 2026; **1 GW only Jan 2028** (not “three years” press). Ajman 100 MW slipped >1 year
- Power: EIA 2024 mix still ~71% gas, 23% nuclear (Barakah 5.6 GW), 5% solar. A 5 GW AI campus is a **new inhabitability load**, not a software story. Masdar/EWEC “round-the-clock solar + 1 GW gas turbine” (EIA CAB) is the coupling

### Export / sovereignty paradox (S-05, S-91, A10)

G42 got US chips and Microsoft/OpenAI only after dumping China. IISS (Jun 2026): “American permission is a precondition for ostensibly sovereign infrastructure.” Hedging is visible (Mistral, France DCs, AMD in Europe). BIS Country Group upgrade (2026) is reversible. This is C-10 (pause-switch) as a *chip licence*, not a slogan.

### Geospatial the UAE already owns

Do not compete with Space42’s brochure. Use it.

- **GIQ** (UAE Space Agency × Space42): marketplace that already lists **Planet, Maxar, Airbus, BlackSky, Satellogic, Umbra, Sentinel-1/2, Landsat, MBRSC, Space42**. Next-gen launched GITEX Oct 2025
- Planet × UAE Space Agency: Loss & Damage Atlas (COP); SuperDove ~3–5 m daily, SkySat tasking, 30+ TB/day
- Dubai Digital Twin (2026): ~1,500 geospatial layers, ~195k buildings, ~280k infra assets
- Abu Dhabi DMT × Space42 (Oct 2025): AI urban planning

**Implication for projects:** Planet is both an *input we can buy* and a *source already inside GIQ*. Leadership is the **graph + change-detection overlay**, not another imagery portal.

## 2. Ranking method

Each idea scored 1–5 on:

| Axis | Weight | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Observability** | 30% | Can OSINT + gov + Planet/Sentinel/SAR actually see it? |
| **Decision value** | 30% | Updates a residuality node, a chip/campus claim, or a live investment call |
| **Gap vs incumbents** | 20% | Not already Epoch’s Stargate page, GIQ, or G42 IR |
| **Ship v1** | 20% | Outside-in team, weeks to a first artefact, no classified, no people-doxxing |

**Filter:** if it requires royal-court internals, ADIA line items, or G42 OT diagrams, it is ranked out. Same rule as `uae.md`.

### 2. Stargate / Khazna physical-truth layer (Planet + Sentinel + SAR)

**Score: 4.5** — obs 5, decision 5, gap 3, ship 5

Epoch already proved the method (clearing 9 Jun 2025 → partial buildings 16 Nov 2025 → 1 GW median Jan 2028 vs press “three years”). **Extend it from one campus to the fleet, and from “buildings exist” to “power and water can feed them.”**

**Sensors**
- **Planet SuperDove** (daily 3–5 m): earthworks, roof kit, parking/workforce proxy, laydown yards
- **Planet SkySat / Pelican** (sub-meter tasking): crane counts, transformer pads, cooling plant
- **Sentinel-2** (free, 10 m, 5-day): baseline and burn-down if Planet budget is tight
- **Sentinel-1 / Space42 Foresight SAR**: all-weather, night, dust; 25 cm claimed — construction through haboob/war
- **VIIRS / Landsat thermal + night lights**: when a hall is actually drawing power (uptime), not just roofed
- **AIS / ADS-B**: heavy-lift and transformer deliveries at Khalifa / Jebel Ali / AUH

**Sites to watch (minimum set)**
1. Stargate UAE 5 GW campus (Abu Dhabi) — 19.2 km²; 200 MW = buildings 1–2
2. Khazna Ajman 100 MW (already late)
3. Khazna Masdar 32 MW
4. Next 100 MW halls if Epoch’s “no more buildings as of Nov 2025” breaks
5. Cooling/desal and transmission corridors into the campus (project 3)

**Decision it takes:** press “Q3 2026 200 MW live” vs steel. Directly updates S-91 (US partner pause is worthless if the building is empty) and C-13 (compute as a single door).

**Gap vs Epoch:** they stop at IT-MW and chip-eq. You add *energy and water in-edges* and the rest of Khazna. Gap vs GIQ: GIQ sells imagery; it does not publish an adversarial completion curve.

**v1:** monthly Planet subscription over two AOIs (Stargate + Ajman) + a one-page “MW implied by roofed halls.”

### 3. Compute × power × water coupling graph (A5 meets AI)

**Score: 4.4** — obs 4, decision 5, gap 5, ship 3

The residuality finding: inhabitability is the chaotic node. A 5 GW campus is a new customer of that node. EIA: UAE is still a **net gas importer** (Dolphin from Qatar). Barakah is 5.6 GW nameplate — one Stargate-scale campus is in the same units as the country’s nuclear plant.

**Build:** a subgraph of project 1 where every DC node *must* have `powered_by` and `cooled_by` / `watered_by` edges, each with a satellite-checkable plant.

**Sources:** EWEC plant list, DEWA, TAQA, ENEC/FANR, EIA CAB, Masdar 5.2 GW solar+storage+1 GW gas (EIA), Fujairah F3 2.4 GW CCGT (east-coast — correlated with C-02 geography). Planet/Sentinel on (a) new substations and OHTL toward the campus, (b) desal/cooling towers, (c) solar field progress at MBR and Al Dhafra.

**Decision it takes:** whether C-01 can absorb AI load without stealing hospital/desal electrons in August (S-46/S-69). If the campus is islanded on its own CCGT+solar, that is a residue; if it rides the national peak, it is a coupling.

**v1:** one Sankey: 2026 200 MW → which plants; 5 GW → which plants *do not yet exist*.

### 4. Dual-source EO bus (Planet optical + Foresight/ICEYE SAR + Sentinel)

**Score: 4.1** — obs 5, decision 4, gap 4, ship 4

UAE already put Planet *inside GIQ*. Leadership is a **reproducible pipeline** that treats GIQ as one vendor among equals and always dual-keys.

**Pattern (reuse on every physical node):**
1. Sentinel-2 weekly change mask (free tripwire)
2. Planet daily for confirmation and workforce/yard activity
3. SAR (Sentinel-1 free; Foresight/Umbra paid) when optical is blinded (dust, night, war)
4. SkySat only on the week a claim must be falsified (“200 MW live”)

**Also pointed at residuality sites, not only DCs:** desal clusters (C-01 dispersal — still dark), Fujairah tanks/ADCOP, Jebel Ali, Barakah perimeter (public imagery only; no targeting product), 2024 flood replay vs 2026 storm.

**v1:** one notebook: Stargate + one desal cluster + Fujairah, three sensors, change dates.
